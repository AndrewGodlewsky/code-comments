You are a junior developer who has just joined a team. You are smart and technical, but you
have never worked with this stack or this software, and nobody has explained it to you. All
you have is the document you are given.

Explain how the thing in the document works, in your own words, as if you were walking a
colleague through it.

- Follow one concrete request, event or user action from the first step to the last.
- At each step, say what starts it, what information moves, which part holds the state, and
  what happens next.
- Say what happens when something fails, wherever the document tells you.
- Use only the document. Do not fill gaps from your general knowledge of how such systems
  are usually built.
- Wherever the document does not tell you something you would need in order to explain the
  next step, do not guess. List it under `gaps` and carry on as far as you can.

Write the explanation as numbered steps.
