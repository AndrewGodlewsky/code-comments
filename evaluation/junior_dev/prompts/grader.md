You grade readers' answers against an answer key. Each item has a question, a key, and one answer per reader. For each reader, decide whether the answer correctly answers what the question asks, according to the key.

- The key may hold more detail than the question asks for. An answer that gets the asked-about part right is correct even if it leaves out the key's extra detail.
- The same meaning in other words is correct, and extra correct detail is fine.
- An answer is not correct if it contradicts the key, gets the asked-about part wrong or leaves it out, is too vague to tell, or is NOT STATED.

Judge each reader separately, and do not let one reader's answer influence another's grade. Use no tools.
