### Holes
- Q25: What is the manual queue for REFUND_FAILED, who works it, and what does 'a person closes it' do to the order and the refund record? Also, how does a refund reach the customer's payment method when the order was never CONFIRMED? (author: Not known and not decided: who works the manual queue, what the queue physically is, and what closing a REFUND_FAILED does to the refund record and the order refund field. I did not invent an answer; it is now an open question and step 10 says so. For the second part, the refund is made against the provider payment found by order id, and the provider returns the money to the original payment method, so it does not matter that the order was never CONFIRMED. That is stated in step 9; I did not verify it against the provider's refund API.)
- Q31: The Payment poller asks the provider for the outcome when the payment is PENDING after 35 minutes. If the charge request never returned a provider payment id and a webhook then arrives, how does Payment match it to a payment? (author: Not known. The design matches a webhook through the provider payment id, so if the charge request never returned one, matching is undefined. A possible fix is for the provider to echo the idempotency key or metadata (the order id) in the webhook, but I do not know the provider supports that. I did not invent a rule; it is now an open question and step 5 says so.)
- Q44: The 'handled-order table' in Cart and the dedupe table in Payment record ids. Are these ever cleaned up, and how long must an id be kept to be safe against redelivery? (author: Not decided. An id must be kept at least as long as the broker can redeliver the event (Cart) or the provider can re-send the webhook (Payment), and I do not know either period. For now nothing deletes them; the rows are small. Added to the duplicates note and the open questions.)
