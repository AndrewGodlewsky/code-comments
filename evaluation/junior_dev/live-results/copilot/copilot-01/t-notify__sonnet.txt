I wrote the design to `docs/design/notifications.md`. No code was changed.

An operator runs `python -m src.shipping <order_id> <carrier> <tracking>`, which marks the order shipped, saves it, then emails the customer. The shipment is saved before the email is tried, so a failed email never undoes it. `--resend-pending` retries orders whose email failed.

The design assumes some choices the codebase doesn't make yet:
- **No shipping step today:** orders have no status and no shipped state.
- **No customer address:** `place_order` would take a new required `email` argument. Old orders have none, so they are never emailed.
- **README receipt claim:** it is wrong, since nothing sends email today. I left it out of scope.

A junior-dev review took 2 rounds, and a fresh reader then explained the design back correctly. Its remaining gaps were minor: who sets the initial `status` and `ship_email_sent` values at order time, and whether `mark_shipped` or `send_shipped_email` saves the flag. I did not rewrite the file after that check.

One hole remains open: the email provider isn't chosen, so its exact settings aren't specified. Only the rule that credentials come from environment variables is.
